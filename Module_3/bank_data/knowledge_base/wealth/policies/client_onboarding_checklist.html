Wealth Client Onboarding Checklist

Owner: Wealth Operations · Applies to: all NovaBank Wealth staff

New wealth clients need a minimum investable surplus of Rs 1 crore. The advisor completes a risk-profiling questionnaire, records the client's investment horizon and assigns one of the three model portfolios.

Source-of-wealth documents (tax returns for 3 years or audited business accounts) are mandatory before the first investment. Politically exposed persons need approval from the Head of Compliance.
