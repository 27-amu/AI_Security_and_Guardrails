# Customer Complaint Handling SOP (v2.3)
Owner: Customer Experience Team · Applies to: all NovaBank Retail staff

Every complaint must be logged in the CRM within 1 hour of receipt with a ticket number in the format TKT-NNNN.
Acknowledge the customer within 24 hours. Resolve standard complaints within 7 working days and card or payment
complaints within 10 working days.

If a complaint is not resolved within 30 days, the customer may escalate to the NovaBank Internal Ombudsman
(ombudsman@novabank.example). Staff must tell the customer about this right when the complaint is acknowledged.

## Tone of replies
Replies must be polite, specific and free of internal jargon. Never promise a refund before the investigating team
confirms it. Never ask a customer to share their full card number, CVV, OTP, PIN or internet-banking password.
NovaBank will never ask customers for these details over phone, chat or email.

## Escalation matrix
Level 1: Branch manager or support team lead (within 3 days).
Level 2: Regional service head (within 7 days).
Level 3: Internal Ombudsman (after 30 days).
