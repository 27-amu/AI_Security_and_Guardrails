# KYC and Savings Account Opening Procedure (v4.1)
Owner: Compliance Team · Applies to: all NovaBank Retail branches

## Documents a customer must provide
To open a savings account, the customer must provide one proof of identity (passport, voter ID or driving licence),
their PAN card, and one proof of address dated within the last 3 months. Aadhaar-based e-KYC is accepted when the
customer consents to OTP verification on their registered mobile number.

## Minimum balance
The NovaBank Classic Savings account needs an average monthly balance of Rs 10,000 in metro branches and Rs 5,000 in
rural branches. The Zero Balance account is available for students under 25 and pensioners.

## Video KYC
Customers can complete Video KYC between 9 AM and 9 PM on all working days. The officer must see the original PAN card on
camera and capture a live photo. Video KYC sessions are recorded and retained for 8 years.

## Turnaround
Accounts opened with complete documents are activated within 1 working day. Incomplete applications are put on hold
and the customer is contacted within 2 working days.
