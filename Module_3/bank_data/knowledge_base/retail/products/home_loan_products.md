# Home Loan Products (v2026.2)
Owner: Retail Lending Team · Applies to: all NovaBank Retail staff

NovaBank Home Loans are offered from Rs 5 lakh to Rs 5 crore for up to 30 years. The floating rate starts at
8.45% for salaried borrowers with a credit score above 750 and 8.75% for self-employed borrowers.
Processing fee is 0.5% of the loan amount, capped at Rs 15,000.

Eligibility: the borrower must be 21 to 65 years old at loan maturity. The maximum loan-to-value ratio is 90% for
loans up to Rs 30 lakh, 80% up to Rs 75 lakh and 75% above that.

There is no prepayment penalty on floating-rate home loans for individual borrowers.
