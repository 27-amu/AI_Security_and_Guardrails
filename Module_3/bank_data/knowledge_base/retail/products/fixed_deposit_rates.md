# Fixed Deposit Interest Rates — effective 1 October 2026
Owner: Treasury Team · Applies to: all NovaBank Retail staff

| Tenure | General public | Senior citizens (60+) |
|---|---|---|
| 7 to 45 days | 3.50% | 4.00% |
| 46 days to 6 months | 5.25% | 5.75% |
| 6 months 1 day to 1 year | 6.40% | 6.90% |
| 1 year 1 day to 2 years | 7.10% | 7.60% |
| 2 years 1 day to 5 years | 6.90% | 7.40% |

Premature withdrawal attracts a penalty of 1% on the applicable rate. The minimum deposit is Rs 5,000.
Tax is deducted at source (TDS) when interest crosses Rs 40,000 a year (Rs 50,000 for senior citizens) unless the
customer submits Form 15G or 15H.
