# Fraud Monitoring Thresholds — CONFIDENTIAL
Owner: Risk Team · Visible to: risk department only

Card transactions above Rs 49,999 from a new device are held for manual review. More than 5 UPI transfers to new
beneficiaries within 1 hour triggers a temporary block. International card-not-present transactions above
USD 800 are declined unless the customer has enabled international usage in the last 24 hours.
These thresholds must never be shared outside the risk team because fraudsters can use them to stay under the limits.
