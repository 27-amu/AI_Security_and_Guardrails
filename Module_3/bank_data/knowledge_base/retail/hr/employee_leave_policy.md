# Employee Leave Policy (2026)
Owner: Human Resources · Applies to: all NovaBank Retail employees

Every employee gets 24 days of privilege leave, 12 days of sick leave and 8 days of casual leave per calendar year.
Up to 30 days of unused privilege leave can be carried forward. Branch staff must apply for planned leave at least
7 days in advance through the HR portal. Employees in cash-handling roles must take a block of at least 5
consecutive days of leave once a year (mandatory leave rule, a fraud-control measure).
