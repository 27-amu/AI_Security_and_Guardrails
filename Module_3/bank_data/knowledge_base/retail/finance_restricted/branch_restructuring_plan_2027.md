# Branch Restructuring Plan 2027 — RESTRICTED (Board only)
Owner: Office of the CFO · Visible to: executive committee only

The Board approved closing 14 under-performing branches in Q1 2027, mainly in Pune, Nagpur and Indore.
About 320 roles will be affected. 180 employees will be offered transfers; the remaining roles will be covered by a
voluntary retirement scheme. Do not communicate this to staff before the official announcement on 15 January 2027.
Expected annual cost saving: Rs 96 crore. Branch closures must be notified to the regulator 90 days in advance.
